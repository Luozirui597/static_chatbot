# S1 — B fixed — Direct fact and unsupported relation
# Condition: B
# Expected model calls: 2
# Focus: B: Distinguish direct teaching from an unestablished relation; never infer.

## T1 (teach)
We are doing a short fictional logic puzzle. A, B, C, and D are only labels. In this puzzle, "links" is a made-up relation; do not use outside knowledge about links.

I teach you these facts:
- A links to B.
- B links to C.
- A links to D.
Both A-B and B-C are marked active.

Puzzle rule: for these tokens, if X links to Y and Y links to Z, and both links are marked active, then X links to Z.

## Q1 (query)
Answer three questions using only what I taught:
1. Does A link to D? Was that taught directly, inferred, or can you not tell?
2. Does A link to C? Was that taught directly, inferred, or can you not tell?
3. Does C link to A? Was that taught directly, inferred, or can you not tell?
For anything inferred, give the premises and the rule you used.
