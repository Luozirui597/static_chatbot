# S3 — Relation facts without a combination rule

Kind: fixed
Calls: 2
Focus: Do not assume transitivity; the combination rule has not been provided.

## T1 (teach)

We are doing a short fictional logic puzzle. A, B, and C are only labels. In this puzzle, "links" is a made-up relation; do not use outside knowledge about links.

I teach you these facts:
- A links to B.
- B links to C.

## Q1 (query)

Does A link to C? Use only what I taught. If you need a rule to combine the links, say what you need.
